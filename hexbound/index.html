Combat & movement rules

Stacks and movement: 1-piece Scouts move up to 4 squares, 2-piece Warriors 3, and 3-piece Juggernauts 2. Movement uses all eight directions; stacks cannot move through occupied squares.

Attack: Attack one adjacent enemy stack from any of the eight directions. Attack power equals the attacking stack's size plus up to two flanking support directions. Defence equals the target's size plus up to two friendly guard directions. If power exceeds defence, deal 1 damage; if power is at least 2 higher, deal 2. Ties are repelled. Each attack costs 1 AP and activates the attacker.

Flanking: Friendly stacks adjacent to the target from different directions add attack power. The target's adjacent allies add defence. Surrounding an enemy can make even a small stack dangerous; keeping allies nearby makes isolated attacks harder.

Push / Sumito: Select a stack and push an adjacent enemy orthogonally. Up to three contiguous friendly stacks behind the attacker combine their mass. They must strictly outnumber up to three contiguous enemy stacks. The enemy line moves one square; an occupied landing square blocks it. A stack pushed beyond the board is eliminated. All friendly stacks in the pushing line activate together.

Turns: Each player receives 3 action points per turn. A stack can act once per round. Win by eliminating all opposing pieces. No objective-control shortcut.
