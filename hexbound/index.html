Rules reference

Each round players alternate turns with 3 command points. Each stack activates once per round. Stack size is mass and push strength: a 1-piece Scout moves 4 hexes, a 2-piece Warrior moves 3, and a 3-piece Juggernaut moves 2.

Abalone-style sumito: a straight, contiguous line of up to three friendly stacks can combine their total mass to push a smaller contiguous enemy line. You must outnumber the enemy mass; equal strength cannot push. Occupied spaces block a push. If a pushed stack is forced off the board, that entire stack is eliminated.

Eliminate every enemy piece to win. Move, push, merge adjacent friendly stacks up to 3 pieces, or split one piece into an adjacent empty hex.
