Rules

Movement: Scouts move 5, Warriors 4, Juggernauts 3. Each side has 24 team movement points per turn; moving costs one point per hex.

Facing and attacks: Three green facets show the front, two red facets the sides, and one dark facet the rear. Side attacks damage; rear attacks instantly eliminate. Front and front-oblique attacks are illegal.

Split and merge: Adjacent friendly stacks can split or merge (maximum stack size 3). Each operation costs two team points.

Push: Up to three contiguous friendly stacks combine their mass and must strictly outnumber the enemy line. Occupied landing hexes block a push; stacks pushed off-board are eliminated.

Victory: Eliminate all opposing pieces.
