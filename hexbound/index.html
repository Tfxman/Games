The board is a hexagon made of 91 flat-top hex tiles arranged in 11 rows. The six outer edges form one continuous large hexagon. Movement follows the six hex directions. Each piece is a flat-top hexagon with six distinct directional panels: three forward panels are green (safe), two rear-side panels are red (damage angles), and the rear panel is black (instant elimination). The panel colors rotate with the stack's facing. Only side attacks can damage; rear attacks eliminate the entire stack. Front and front-oblique attacks are illegal. Sumito pushes follow hex lines.

Combat & movement rules

Stacks and movement: Armies field 36 pieces each. Scouts (1 piece) move up to 5 hexes, Warriors (2) 4, and Juggernauts (3) 3. Each side has 24 movement points per turn; every square moved costs one team point and one point from that stack's personal movement allowance. A unit's allowance refreshes at the start of its side's next turn.

Facing and attack: The colored panels show a unit's facing. Attack from either side to deal damage (1 or 2 based on relative mass and support). Attack from directly behind to instantly eliminate the entire stack. Front and front-oblique attacks are illegal and consume no movement. Each attack costs 1 team movement point and 1 point from the attacking unit's personal allowance. Rotate with the buttons; moving changes facing toward the direction of travel.

Split and merge: Split a stack into two smaller stacks by choosing a piece count and clicking an empty adjacent square. Merge adjacent friendly stacks when their combined size is no more than three. Each operation costs one personal movement point from each participating stack and two team movement points.

Push / Sumito: Up to three contiguous friendly stacks combine their mass and must strictly outnumber the enemy line. All friendly and enemy stacks involved shift one square in the push direction and turn to face that direction. Every friendly stack in the pushing line spends one personal movement point; the team pays one point per friendly stack. Occupied landing squares block the push; stacks pushed off-board are eliminated.

Turns: Each player receives 24 team movement points per turn. Movement distance, attacks, rotations, splitting, merging, and pushes consume movement from both the team pool and the relevant units' individual allowances. Unspent movement does not carry over. Win by eliminating all opposing pieces.
