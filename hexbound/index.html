Quick rules

61 hexes, three central objectives. Each side deploys 12 pieces in its two home rows, in stacks of up to three. Stack size is its mass, health, and push strength. Scouts move 2, Warriors 3, Juggernauts 4 hexes.

Each round, players alternate activations and receive 3 command points. A unit can activate once per round. Front attacks deal no damage; a stronger attacker may push. Side attacks remove one piece, with a stronger attacker also pushing. A rear attack eliminates the target if the attacker was already in the rear hex when its activation began. Pushing into an occupied hex is blocked; pushing off the board eliminates the stack.

At round end, control two of three objectives for two consecutive round checks to win. Eliminating all enemy pieces also wins. Rotate changes facing; merge combines adjacent friendly stacks up to three; split moves the top piece to an adjacent empty hex.
