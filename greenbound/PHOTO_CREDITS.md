## Image interpretation notes

Photographs show particular specimens and seasonal stages, not every diagnostic feature of a species. The milkweed photograph shows mature seed pods; the blueberry photograph shows flowers; the plantain photograph does not show its flower spike; and the yarrow photograph is a close view of flowering heads. The game therefore asks players to compare multiple characters and habitat notes rather than rely on a single image.

Photographs are visual learning aids, not safe-foraging guidance. Never taste or handle an unknown plant.
