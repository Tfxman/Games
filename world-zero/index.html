How to play

Choose a Japanese response from the fixed options in each scene, then choose an action. Several natural responses may be correct, and incorrect options are included so you can learn from context.

Challenges are deterministic: your relevant ability modifier is compared with the challenge difficulty. No dice, AI service, API key, or internet connection is required.
